4-Stage Signal Filtering

Raw ECG signals contain baseline wander, EMI noise, and muscle artifacts. The pipeline cleans the signal before UI display while keeping the raw signal for the SVC model.

Before Raw Signal Baseline wander · EMI noise · Artifacts
After Filtered Signal 0.5–40Hz · Normalized [−1, 1]
1

Baseline Removal

Two-pass median filter — 200ms window then 600ms window. Removes slow baseline wander without distorting P/T waves.

2

Bandpass Filter

Zero-phase Butterworth SOS, order 4, 0.5–40Hz. Removes residual drift and high-freq EMI while preserving QRS, P, T morphology.

3

Savitzky-Golay

Window=21 samples (~84ms), polyorder=3. Smooths sharp R-peaks while preserving overall waveform morphology.

4

Normalization

Min-Max normalization to [−1, 1] range. Ensures consistent amplitude scale for waveform visualization across all patients.
